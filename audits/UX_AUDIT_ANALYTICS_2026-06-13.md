# UX Audit — Advanced Business Analytics (MK Cycles Dashboard)

**Date:** 2026-06-13  ·  **Branch:** TALLY-V2  ·  **Method:** 8 parallel UX finder agents across the analytics surface; high/critical findings merit-checked by an independent reviewer (some merit-checks were rate-limited and those findings are carried through unverified).

> Framing question for every finding: **can a non-technical distributor/owner reach a decision (reorder, pricing, who-to-call, cash position) quickly and correctly from dense Tally data?**

---
## Executive Summary

Across the eight analytics areas, **99 findings** were raised and **99 retained** after merit-checking (0 dismissed). The surface is feature-rich and data-complete, but the recurring theme is that it **presents data more than it supports decisions**: absolute numbers without trends/deltas or comparison-to-prior-period, dense tables where a ranked or visual summary would answer the question faster, inconsistent filtering/time-range patterns page-to-page, and accessibility/number-formatting gaps (contrast, keyboard, ₹ lakh/crore, tabular-nums) that slow scanning. The highest-severity items cluster in **decision-support gaps** (Dashboard/KPI, Items, Outreach) and **error-prone pricing interactions** (Invoices/Pricing).

## ⚡ Quick Wins (high impact ÷ small effort)

- **`reports-04` — ₹ formatter has no Crore tier — large figures render as unreadable 'Lakh' strings** (🟠 HIGH): Add a Crore tier to formatINR and make it the canonical helper. Concretely: const a=Math.abs(n); if(a>=1_00_00_000) return `${n<0?'-':''}Rs ${(Math.abs(n)/1_00_00_000).toFixed(2)} Cr`; if(a>=1_00_000) return `${n<0?'-':''}Rs ${(Math.abs(n)/1_00_000).toFixed(2)} L`; else grouped en-IN. Compute the sign once and apply to the absolute value so negatives (Gross Profit when red, or a negative bank balance) read "-Rs 2.00 L" rather than "Rs -2.00 L". Promote this Cr/L-tiered version into src/utils/format.ts (the existing fmtINR there has no Cr/L tier -- extend it, do not just import it as-is) and delete the local copy in Reports.tsx so Dashboard/other pages format identically. Keep full grouped digits in CSV/PDF exports and in drill-down line items where exact figures matter; the Cr/L collapse should be reserved for KPI headline cards and chart axes/tooltips where scannability beats precision.
- **`ia-09` — Predicted Orders mixes a 12-month average with a 'last 3 months' claim — misleading reorder math** (🟠 HIGH): Reconcile the two: either compute avgOut over a genuine trailing 3 months (use avgMonthlyOutwardIndexed with nMonths=3, already in inventory.ts:207) and keep the caption, or change the caption to '12-month average demand'. #### `dataviz-05` — Cash-flow prediction confidence band is drawn as two overlapping baseline Areas, not a true band

- **Severity:** 🟠 HIGH  ·  **Effort:** M  ·  **Heuristic:** Truthful representation of uncertainty
- **Location:** src/pages/reports/CashflowIntelligence.tsx:127-135
- **Problem:** The 30-day forecast renders `<Area dataKey="upperBound" fill="#2563eb" fillOpacity={0.1} />` and `<Area dataKey="lowerBound" fill="#2563eb" fillOpacity={0.1} />` as two independent areas, each filling from the chart baseline (0) up to its value. A true confidence band must fill the region BETWEEN lower and upper. As written, the shaded area is just 'everything below the upper bound' double-painted with 'everything below the lower bound' — the visible shading does not represent the uncertainty interval at all, and where net goes negative it breaks entirely.
- **User impact:** The owner reads the shaded region as the range of likely cash outcomes for the next 30 days and plans payments accordingly, but the shading is mathematically wrong — it overstates the band on the downside and is meaningless near zero. This can drive a bad cash decision on a chart explicitly titled '30-Day Cash Flow Prediction'.
- **Recommendation:** Plot the band as a single stacked pair: an invisible Area for `lowerBound` (fill transparent) plus an Area whose dataKey is the (upper-lower) delta stacked on top, OR precompute a `[lower, upper]` tuple and use a single Area with a range. Add a legend entry 'Confidence range' and keep the predicted line on top.
- **🔍 Merit-check:** Confirmed against code. CashflowIntelligence.tsx:132-134 renders two independent baseline-anchored `<Area>`s (`upperBound`, `lowerBound`), no `stackId`, both fill #2563eb at fillOpacity 0.1 from y=0 to their value. The engine (cashflowEngine.ts:298-308) makes these a CUMULATIVE net projection: predictedNet = avgDailyNet*i, upper/lower = (avgDailyNet ± sd*0.5)*i. Because these are cumulative *net* values, they routinely cross zero and go negative — confirmed by the engine's own 'projected_crunch' warning which fires when day30.lowerBound < -500000. So the designer's diagnosis is technically accurate: the fill is never the lower→upper interval; it is always 'everything between 0 and each bound,' double-painted. Visually two artifacts result: (1) when both bounds are positive, the band-between strip is only faintly lighter than the solid fill running all the way down to 0, so the true interval doesn't read as the band; (2) in the negative/cash-crunch case the lowerBound area fills DOWNWARD from 0 while upperBound fills upward, producing a blob straddling zero that has nothing to do with the uncertainty interval — exactly the scenario that matters most for a cash decision. This is a truthful-representation-of-uncertainty defect on a chart explicitly titled '30-Day Cash Flow Prediction' and wired to a danger-level crunch warning, so it is decision-relevant, not cosmetic. Not invalid, not a preference. Keeping severity HIGH rather than escalating to critical: the chart is on a secondary intelligence page (not the primary Dashboard), the predicted line itself is plotted correctly on top, and the worst-case crunch figure is also surfaced as an explicit numeric warning, so a user is not solely dependent on reading the (broken) shading. The misleading band is a real risk but has a textual safety net.
- **Sharper recommendation:** Do NOT just slap stackId on the two existing Areas — Recharts stacked areas mishandle the negative-baseline case here (cumulative net crosses zero), so stacking lower + delta still misbehaves where lowerBound < 0. The robust fix: precompute a single tuple field per point in generate30DayPrediction, e.g. `band: [lowerBound, upperBound]`, and render ONE area: `<Area type="monotone" dataKey="band" stroke="none" fill="#2563eb" fillOpacity={0.12} name="Confidence range" />`. Recharts supports array dataKeys for a true range area that fills strictly lower→upper regardless of sign. Keep `<Line dataKey="predictedNet">` on top. Three supporting fixes while there: (1) add a real legend/label entry 'Likely range (±0.5σ)' since the band currently has no legend and ±0.5σ is a narrow, undocumented interval the user can't interpret; (2) the Tooltip currently formats every series including the raw bounds with fmtINR but gives no min/max framing — add a custom tooltip that shows 'Predicted ₹X (range ₹low–₹high)'; (3) consider widening to ±1σ or labeling the confidence level, because 0.5σ understates uncertainty and the same band feeds the cash-crunch danger warning. Effort is M as stated — most of it is the engine tuple + one tooltip; the chart JSX shrinks from two Areas to one.

### Cross-cutting Interaction, States & Accessibility

> **Assessment:** The shared shell (NavBar, Toast, ErrorBoundary, Layout) and the design-system CSS (search-input, filter-chip, tab-item, empty-state, KPICard) are solid and used in most pages — but cross-cutting consistency breaks down badly at the page level. Three systemic problems dominate. (1) Color contrast: the custom Tailwind `neutral` palette is shifted far lighter than standard Tailwind — `neutral-500 = #a1a1a6` (~2.3:1 on white), `neutral-600 = #8e8e93` (~3.0:1) — yet these are the tokens used everywhere for secondary/muted text, frequently at 11px (`text-2xs`, 94 occurrences). This fails WCAG AA across virtually every page and directly contradicts the MEMORY.md claim of a 7.2:1 fix. (2) Number formatting is inconsistent: `fmtINR` (the canonical formatter) emits full rupee figures with no lakh/crore abbreviation, while Reports renders "₹x.xx L", Calendar renders "₹xCr/₹xK", Dashboard divides by 100000 inline, and Settings hand-rolls "/100000 L" — so the same KPI reads differently page to page. (3) Two of the heaviest analytics pages, Reports and Dashboard, ignore the design system entirely: Reports defines its own KPICard, its own formatINR, its own tab bar, an inaccessible drill-down modal (no role/dialog, no Escape, no focus trap), a different empty state, and a non-standard sticky `max-w-7xl` shell; DistancePage/PriceList use raw slate/blue colors. Loading and empty states are also non-uniform — `KPISkeleton` and `ErrorCard` exist but are never imported by any page, so heavy data loads show either zeros or a bare spinner, and no analytics page surfaces a recoverable error state. Tabs everywhere are plain buttons with no `role="tablist"/tab"/aria-selected`, hurting screen-reader users. None of these block a determined sighted user, but they add friction, erode trust in the numbers, and lock out low-vision and keyboard/AT users.

#### `cc-02` — Currency formatting is inconsistent page-to-page (no shared lakh/crore convention)

- **Severity:** 🟠 HIGH  ·  **Effort:** M  ·  **Heuristic:** Nielsen — Consistency & standards; analytics best practice — one canonical money format
- **Location:** src/utils/format.ts:27-29 (fmtINR, no abbreviation) vs Reports.tsx:51-56 (formatINR → '₹x.xx L') vs Calendar.tsx:109-111 ('₹xCr/₹xK') vs Dashboard.tsx:320 (v/100000+'L') vs Settings.tsx:678-680 ('/100000).toFixed(1)L')
- **Problem:** There are at least four different rupee renderers. The canonical `fmtINR` formats the full number (`₹12,34,567`) with no lakh/crore compaction. Reports redefines its own `formatINR` that switches to '₹12.35 L' above a lakh. Calendar has its own scale ('₹1.2Cr', '₹45K'). Dashboard and Settings inline `/100000` math with an 'L' suffix. So the identical 'Sales' figure appears as `₹12,34,567` on the Dashboard KPI, `₹12.35 L` in Reports, and `₹12K`-style elsewhere.
- **User impact:** A distribution owner comparing the Dashboard headline sales against the Reports tab sees two visually different numbers for the same metric and must mentally reconcile lakhs vs full figures — eroding trust in the dashboard and slowing the cash/sales decisions the pages exist to support.
- **Recommendation:** Add one `fmtINRCompact(n)` to utils/format.ts implementing the Indian convention (₹..K / ₹..L / ₹..Cr with a fixed decimal rule) plus the existing exact `fmtINR`, and delete the per-page `formatINR`/inline `/100000` variants in Reports, Calendar, Dashboard, Settings. #### `cc-09` — Toasts are not announced to screen readers and auto-dismiss too fast for error recovery

- **Severity:** 🟡 MEDIUM  ·  **Effort:** S  ·  **Heuristic:** WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages; Nielsen — Visibility of system status & error recovery
- **Location:** src/components/Toast.tsx:23 (4000ms fixed timeout), 29 (region wrapper), 55 (role="alert" per item)
- **Problem:** The toast container is `role="region" aria-label="Notifications"` but is not an `aria-live` region; individual toasts use `role="alert"` only after insertion, which is unreliable for SR announcement, and there is no distinction in politeness between success and error. #### `cc-08` — Filter / time-range controls differ in shape and defaults across pages

- **Severity:** 🟡 MEDIUM  ·  **Effort:** L  ·  **Heuristic:** Nielsen — Consistency & standards; analytics best practice — predictable, persistent time controls
- **Location:** Dashboard.tsx:211-241 (All/Month/Quarter/FY/Custom segmented, default 'all'), Reports.tsx:954-988 (This Month/Last Month/YTD chips + date inputs, default YTD), Invoices.tsx:251-266 (type filter-chips + From/To), Outreach.tsx:993-1000, Calendar.tsx:1096-1122 (multi-select type toggles)
- **Problem:** Every page invents its own period model.
